# Retail360 — Business Requirements
1. Project Information

Project Name: Retail360 — Integrated Supermarket Data Intelligence Platform

**Business:** LankaMart Retail Group
Project Type: Data Engineering & Analytics Platform
Data: Synthetic

2. Business Problem
LankaMart Retail Group operates multiple interconnected business operations including retail stores, inventory, supply chain, bakery production, prepared food, fresh produce, online sales and customer loyalty.
These operations generate data across different processes.Management requires an integrated and reliable analytical platform that can combine these datasets and provide consistent information for operational and strategic decision-making.The Retail360 platform will address this problem by implementing an end-to-end data engineering solution for collecting, validating, transforming, storing and serving business data.

3. Business Objectives
BO-01 — Data Integration
*Integrate data from multiple operational business domains into a centralized analytical platform.
BO-02 — Incremental Processing
*Support the ingestion and processing of new operational data on a daily basis.
BO-03 — Data Quality
*Identify and manage invalid, incomplete, duplicated and inconsistent records before they enter analytical datasets.
BO-04 — Retail Intelligence
Provide trusted analytical datasets for analysing sales, product performance, store performance, customer purchasing behaviour and sales channels.
BO-05 — Inventory Intelligence
*Analyse inventory movements, stock availability, stockouts and product movement.
BO-06 — Production Intelligence
*Analyse bakery and prepared-food production, sales and waste.
BO-07 — Supply Chain Intelligence
*Analyse suppliers, purchasing, distribution and store replenishment.
BO-08 — Customer 360
Combine customer activity across physical and online channels.
BO-09 — Analytical Data Marts
Create subject-oriented datasets for management and operational analytics.
BO-10 — Pipeline Automation
Automate data processing and pipeline orchestration using modern data engineering technologies.

These areas may be considered as future extensions.

6. Expected Outcome

The completed platform should provide a reliable analytical foundation that allows business users to understand supermarket operations across retail, production, inventory, supply chain, customer and online channels.

The platform should demonstrate industry-oriented data engineering practices rather than simple data analysis.

7. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

1. Multiple business domains can be integrated.
2. Data quality problems can be detected and handled.
3. Historical and incremental data can be processed.
4. Data can move through RAW, STAGING and WAREHOUSE layers.
5. Analytical data marts can be produced.
6. Business questions can be answered using SQL.
7. Dashboards can consume trusted analytical data.
8. Pipelines can be automated and monitored.
9. The complete architecture and engineering decisions are documented.
10. The project can be demonstrated as a professional Data Engineering portfolio project.
